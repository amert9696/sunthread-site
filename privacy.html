Privacy Policy

Sunthread · Last updated: 23 September 2026

EnglishTürkçe

In short

Sunthread needs no account, no email and no sign-in. Your gardens, seeds and settings are stored only on your device. The game is free and is supported by optional ads and in-app purchases; the ad partners described below receive limited device data to show and measure ads. We never sell your data.

Stored on your device

Progress, seeds, hints, themes, streaks and settings are saved in the app's own storage on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising

Sunthread shows rewarded ads that you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ads between gardens. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Google may collect: device identifiers (including Apple's advertising identifier, IDFA, only if you allow tracking), coarse location derived from IP address, device and app information, ad interaction data, and diagnostic data. Google uses it to deliver, cap, measure and — where permitted — personalise ads, and to prevent fraud.

Tracking choice. On iOS the game asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, no advertising identifier is shared and ads are not personalised with it. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland a consent form is shown before any personalised ads.

Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. Purchases are validated through RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives your purchase history and a random, anonymous app user ID generated on your device so that items can be delivered and restored. It is not linked to your name or email. RevenueCat privacy policy.

What we do not collect

  • Your name, email, phone number, contacts or photos
  • Precise location
  • Microphone or camera data

Children

Sunthread is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights and deletion

Everything on your device is removed when you delete the app. To ask Google or RevenueCat to delete data linked to your device, use the links above, or write to us and we will help. You can reset your advertising identifier in iOS settings.
